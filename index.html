About Me

I’m a web developer who enjoys building lightweight, functional applications that actually work. I focus on HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I use Firebase to handle authentication and data storage on my projects, with plans to expand to React, React Native, and traditional backend development.


Most of what I know comes from building. I learn by turning ideas into real products, breaking things, fixing them, and understanding why they work. My current main project, MiniSoft Pad, shows this approach, alongside several smaller apps that helped me grow through consistent practice.


I care about clean structure, usability, and steady improvement. I also use modern tools, including AI, as helpers—not shortcuts—to stay productive while still learning deeply. This portfolio is a collection of some of what I’ve built so far and a reflection of my progress as a developer.
